The people behind the work

Different strengths. A shared purpose.

People development since 2010 · Coach since 2021 · 700+ coaching hours

Founder · PCC Coach · Facilitator

Shilpa Jahagirdar

inig↗

Shilpa is a PCC credentialed coach with the International Coaching Federation, with a background in IT and more than ten years of experience in corporate training and leadership development.

Her work spans individual coaching, workshops, facilitated group experiences and emotional wellbeing initiatives. She brings together reflective listening, behavioural learning and a deep interest in people, emotions and the patterns that shape how we live.

Shilpa's practice gave Let's Attune its beginning. She continues to shape its human-led work around spaces where people can pause, understand themselves more deeply and move forward with greater clarity.

Founder · CTO · Mentor

Santosh Jahagirdar

in

Santosh brings more than 24 years of experience in technology, solution architecture and leadership across India, the United States and Singapore. He has designed and delivered complex systems across banking, insurance and ecommerce.

At Let's Attune, he leads the vision, design and development of meCove, combining deep technical knowledge with an understanding of how people use technology in real life.

A developer and lifelong learner at heart, Santosh also mentors working professionals through focused conversations and Let's Attune's 90-day programme. He brings systems thinking, curiosity, compassion and honest guidance to the work.

Privacy at Let's Attune

What you share deserves care.

This policy explains what information we collect through this website, why we need it and the choices you have.

Our promise

We use your information to respond to you and provide the support you ask for. We do not sell your personal information.

Last updated: 27 October 2026

01

Information we collect

When you contact us, register interest or ask about our services, you may share your name, email address, phone number, preferred way to be contacted and the reason you are reaching out. You may also choose to write about a personal or workplace situation.

The website may receive basic technical information such as your browser, device, pages visited and approximate location through website hosting and analytics tools.

02

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and understand what kind of support you are looking for.
  • To arrange a conversation, session, workshop or gathering you have asked about.
  • To keep necessary records of our communication and improve how the website works.
  • To meet legal, safety or professional obligations where they apply.

We ask only for information that is relevant to these purposes. You can withdraw your consent to optional communication at any time.

03

Who may process your information

Your enquiry is received by Let's Attune Private Limited and accessed only by people who need it to respond or provide the requested service.

We use service providers to operate the website. The contact form is processed through Formspree, and website hosting or analytics providers may process limited technical information. These providers act under their own privacy and security terms. We may also disclose information if required by law or when necessary to protect someone from serious harm.

We do not sell your personal information or share it for unrelated advertising.

04

Confidentiality in our work

An enquiry through this website is not the same as beginning a coaching or mentoring engagement. If we work together, the confidentiality, records and boundaries relevant to that service are explained separately before the engagement begins.

We treat what you share with care. Confidentiality may have limited exceptions where disclosure is required by law or there is a serious and immediate concern about safety. Those boundaries will be discussed with you.

05

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information only for as long as it is reasonably needed to respond, provide the requested service, maintain necessary business records or meet a legal obligation. When it is no longer needed, we delete or securely dispose of it.

06

Security and cookies

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from loss, misuse and unauthorised access. No website or online transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so please avoid putting highly sensitive information into the contact form.

The website and its service providers may use essential cookies or similar technology so pages work correctly, and limited analytics to understand website use. We do not use the contact information you submit for unrelated advertising.

07

Your choices

You may ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, correct it, delete it when it is no longer required, or stop optional communication. You may also withdraw consent; this will not affect processing that took place before withdrawal or information we must retain by law.

To make a request, email us using the address below. We may need to verify that the request comes from you.

08

Information about children

This website is not intended to collect personal information directly from children. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to contact us with you. If we learn that information was submitted without appropriate involvement, we will take reasonable steps to remove it.

Questions or requests

Talk to us about your information.

Selected work

Some workshops we have designed and delivered.

This is a selection rather than a complete list. Open a workshop to see who it was for, how it was structured and what participants took away.

01Community · Online · 20 participantsIt's Time to RiseAwareness and resilience during Suicide Awareness Month+

Weekly group learning combined with optional one-to-one coaching across one month. The journey explored inner patterns, levels of listening, grief and change, routes to support, and a wider view of life ahead.

Participants found perspective, a place to speak and insight into what may sit beneath the surface.
02Business school · In person · 50 studentsConfidence in the MomentPreparing for interviews and spontaneous questions+

Four activity-led and interactive hours turned a situation often approached with fear into an active, engaging learning experience.

Participants described it as highly interactive, enjoyable and full of learning.
03Workplace community · In person · Two groups of 40Work, Life and the Boundaries BetweenStress, boundaries and sustainable balance+

Interactive conversation and a practical worksheet created room to examine stress, boundaries, balance and the moments in which we know what matters but still miss the mark.

The participants asked for more sessions like it.
04Open workshop · Online · Two-day experienceWomen's Reset & ReconnectionVoice, purpose and peace+

Across two thoughtful sessions, participants slowed down, spoke about what had gone unnoticed and looked at the chapter ahead with renewed awareness.

“This workshop was awakening.” Participants valued the warmth, reflection and freedom to speak openly.
05NGO members · Online · One monthTaking Dreams to RealityA combination of workshops and coaching sessions+

A month-long online experience combining group workshops with coaching sessions. Participants listened more closely to what was getting in the way, recognised the saboteurs and self-talk holding them back, and found a more honest way to push themselves forward.

A space to turn possibility into a clearer, more purposeful next step.
